Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

OnSticky is a sticky-note app for Windows, made by a-mend (“we”). The short version: your notes never leave your computer, and we never receive anything.

What OnSticky stores, and where

  • Your open notes (their text, formatting, colour, opacity and position on screen) are saved in the %APPDATA%\OnSticky folder on your computer, so they reopen next time.
  • Notes you save as files go wherever you choose. By default that's the OnSticky Notes folder in your user folder.
  • Windows settings: OnSticky registers itself to open .sticky files and, if you turn it on, to start when you sign in. Uninstalling removes these settings.

What OnSticky doesn't do

OnSticky doesn't connect to the internet. It has no accounts and no servers, and it doesn't collect analytics, crash reports or usage data. Nothing you type into OnSticky is sent to us or to anyone else.

Deleting your data

Close a note and choose Don't save to discard it. To remove everything, uninstall OnSticky from Settings → Apps, then delete the %APPDATA%\OnSticky folder and any note files you saved.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the download is served by GitHub. GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We don't use cookies or trackers on this site.

Changes and contact
